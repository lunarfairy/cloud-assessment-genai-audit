# Design analysis of the three-arm cluster-randomised AI-policy trial, version 2: methods note (ADEMP)

All numbers in this note are generated by `make_methods_v2.py` from `results/rct_design_summary_v2.json` and the CSV files in `results/`. No student data exist; every data set is simulated. Version 2 corrects the v1 defect: v1 used four strata in total and t(J-6) at J = 56. The planned trial has four strata within each of two offerings (8 strata nested in offering), so the planned analysis is t(J-10) = t(46).

**Terminology.** A *scenario* is one parameter combination, i.e. one row of `scenario_manifest.csv`. A *contrast cell* is scenario x contrast. There are three contrasts per scenario: B-A and C-A are confirmatory and B-C is exploratory. All six estimators are applied to the same simulated trials.

## A: Aims

For the planned design (J = 28: one offering; J = 56: two offerings), estimate the following for six analyses:

1. Type I error.
2. Holm familywise error rate (FWER).
3. Marginal Holm power for each confirmatory contrast.
4. Probability of rejecting both confirmatory contrasts.
5. Bias.
6. Empirical vs model SE.
7. 95% CI coverage.

Also determine the clusters needed for 80% power at d = 0.3, compute analytic MDES, and assess robustness in ten sensitivity families. The study follows ADEMP (Morris, White & Crowther 2019).

## D: Data-generating mechanisms

### Design and randomisation

- **Clusters:** tutorial groups, mean size 25.
- **J = 28:** one offering, 4 strata of 7 groups. c = 6 (intercept, B, C, 3 stratum dummies); planned df = 22.
- **J = 56:** two offerings x 4 strata of 7, i.e. 8 strata nested in offering. c = 10 (intercept, B, C, 7 stratum dummies; the offering main effect is absorbed by the nested strata); planned df = 46.
- **Equal allocation, 10:9:9 per offering:** each stratum of 7 receives 2-2-2 plus one extra group. The four extras of an offering are {A, A, B, C}, assigned to the four strata in random order.
- **Control-heavy allocation, 12:8:8 per offering:** 3:2:2 in every stratum.
- **Per-replicate randomisation:** every simulated trial draws a fresh allocation, i.e. a random permutation of the extras over the strata of each offering and a random permutation of the arm labels over the groups of each stratum. The allocation stream is a SeedSequence child separate from the data stream.
- **Why per-replicate randomisation matters:** it is required for S9, where tutors are fixed to groups and are crossed with arms only through randomisation. In scenarios with exchangeable strata it is equal in distribution to a fixed layout.
- **Sample-size grid:** J/2 groups per offering. Each offering is split into 4 near-equal strata with floor(size/3) groups per arm per stratum; the remaining extras are the cyclic multiset A, B, C, A, ..., assigned at random with no stratum receiving two extras of the same arm.

### Outcome model

The outcome is standardised to total SD 1. The pre-test x has the same ICC as the outcome.

    x_ij = sqrt(rho) a_j + sqrt(1-rho) b_ij
    y_ij = tau_arm(j) + sqrt(rho R2b) a_j + sqrt((1-rho) R2w) b_ij + s_arm [sqrt(rho(1-R2b)) u_j + sqrt((1-rho)(1-R2w)) e_ij]

In the base grid R2b = R2w = R2 and s_arm = 1, which reduces exactly to the v1 model.

**Cluster sizes:** 25 (CV 0) or round(Gamma(1/CV^2, 25 CV^2)), clipped to [12, 75].

**Attrition:** 10% MCAR.

**Base grid:** ICC {0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15} x R2 {0, 0.3, 0.5} x CV {0, 0.3} x d {0, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4} (d for both B and C vs A) x allocation {equal, control-heavy} x J {28, 56}. Replications: 2,000 for null scenarios, 1,000 otherwise.

**Partial null:** dB in {0.3, 0.4}, dC = 0, ICC {0.05, 0.10}, both J and both allocations; 2,000 replications.

### Sensitivity families

All sensitivity families run at J = 56 with equal allocation, ICC 0.05 and 0.10, R2 0.3, CV 0.3, d = 0.3 and 10% MCAR attrition unless the family varies the parameter. Each family has a null version (2,000 replications) where meaningful and an alternative (1,000 replications).

- **S1:** pre-test R2 differs by level: within 0.3, between 0 or 0.6.
- **S2:** total R2 0.1 and 0.2.
- **S3:** bounded 0-100 outcome, raw = 80.78 + 15 y, rounded to 5-point steps and censored at 100. The ceiling sits at the control-arm 90th percentile. The analysis is on the raw scale and the truth is computed by exact integration.
- **S4:** complete-case analysis under three missingness mechanisms. MAR on the pre-test: logit P(miss) = a0 -1.0 x. MNAR on the outcome: logit P(miss) = a0 -1.0 y. In both, a0 is calibrated to 10% at the arm-A null. Differential MCAR: A 10%, B 15%, C 20%.
- **S5:** three variants, each with 10% attrition on top. (i) Cluster-varying consent, p_j ~ Beta with mean 0.75 and SD 0.1. (ii) Arm-differential consent with selection on the pre-test: marginal rates A/B/C = 0.65/0.8/0.8, logit slope 0.5 per SD. (iii) Loss of two randomly chosen whole clusters (J_obs = 54, df = J_obs - c = 44).
- **S6:** arm-C residual SD 1.2 times the other arms.
- **S7:** unequal or opposite effects (dB, dC) = (0.3, 0.15), (0.3, -0.3), (0.3, 0). FWER is computed under the partial null.
- **S8:** offering shift +0.3 SD in offering 2, and arm-by-offering heterogeneity with effects 0.4 (offering 1) and 0.2 (offering 2), truth 0.3. As an extra null check (not requested), effects +0.1 and -0.1 (mean 0).
- **S9:** 14 tutors each teach 4 groups, 2 per offering, crossed with arms by randomisation. The tutor variance share is 0.03 of total variance: y = tau + sqrt(0.97)(y0 - tau) + sqrt(0.03) v_tutor. The data are analysed by the planned model, which ignores tutors.
- **S10:** generalised non-compliance. A fraction c in {0.2, 0.4} of arm-A students uses AI covertly, with effect delta in {-0.3, 0, +0.3} relative to compliant A, so the ITT B-A and C-A contrasts equal d - c delta. Separately, a fraction 0.2 of arm B uses unrestricted AI and receives arm C's effect, so the ITT B-A contrast is dB + 0.2(dC - dB), for (dB, dC) = (0.3, 0.3), (0.3, 0.15), (0.3, 0).

**Sample size:** equal allocation, d = 0.3, J even and split equally over two offerings; J in 36-56 (ICC 0.05) and 56-84 (ICC 0.10); 2,000 replications.

**Seeds:** numpy PCG64, data stream SeedSequence(20261104, spawn_key=(crc32(seed_key),)) and allocation stream spawn_key=(crc32(seed_key), 1). For base and partial-null scenarios, seed_key is the v1 scenario id, so the data streams equal v1. Each scenario's seed is listed in `scenario_manifest.csv`.

## E: Estimands

The estimand is the ITT difference in mean outcome: B-A and C-A (confirmatory) and B-C (exploratory). It is in SD units, except in S3, which uses raw 0-100 points; there d = 0.3 corresponds to 3.921 points.

## M: Methods (estimators)

All six estimators use the fixed-effect design X = [1, B, C, stratum dummies, pre-test].

1. **OLS-HC1 ignoring clustering:** individual-level OLS with HC1 SEs, t on N - p df.
2. **LMM Wald z:** REML random-intercept LMM with a Wald z test.
3. **LMM t(J-c) (planned):** the same REML fit with t on J_obs - c df, where c = 6 at J = 28 and 10 at J = 56. This is the planned primary analysis.
4. **CR2 with t(J-c):** OLS with CR2 (Bell-McCaffrey bias-reduced linearisation) SEs, t on J_obs - c df.
5. **CR2 with Bell-McCaffrey df:** the same CR2 SEs with the Bell-McCaffrey df nu = (tr B)^2 / tr(B^2) under a working-independence model.
6. **Two-stage cluster-level analysis:** stage 1 is individual OLS of y on [1, strata, pre-test], ignoring arm. Stage 2 is unweighted OLS of the cluster-mean residuals on [1, B, C, strata], with t on J_obs - c df.

**Holm procedure** on (p_BA, p_CA): reject the smaller p-value if it is <= 0.025, then the other if it is <= 0.05.

**Implementation:** REML is computed from cluster sufficient statistics (profiled; 31-point grid on rho_r in [0, 0.6] plus 22 golden-section steps). CR2 uses exact 2x2 per-cluster algebra.

## P: Performance measures

Each measure is reported with its Monte Carlo SE (MC SE):

- Rejection rate, SE sqrt(p(1-p)/R).
- Coverage, binomial SE.
- Bias, SE SD/sqrt(R).
- Empirical SE, SE SD/sqrt(2(R-1)).
- Root-mean model SE.
- Holm-adjusted marginal power per contrast.
- holm_both (probability of rejecting both contrasts).
- holm_any.
- FWER over the true-null confirmatory contrasts.

At 2,000 replications the MC SE at 0.05 is 0.0049; the 95% MC band is 0.0404-0.0596.

## Validation

**REML vs statsmodels:** compared on 60 simulated data sets: 30 at J = 56 with 8 nested strata (of which 10 had two lost clusters) and 30 at J = 28. statsmodels 0.15.0 MixedLM was fitted by REML; the best finite-likelihood fit over lbfgs, bfgs and nm was kept. Results:

- Fixed effects: max abs diff 6.9e-07.
- Cluster variance: max abs diff 1.8e-06.
- Residual variance: max abs diff 7.1e-07.
- Arm SEs: relative diff <= 1.6e-05 vs the GLS SE evaluated at statsmodels' variance estimates.
- statsmodels' Hessian-based bse_fe differs from the GLS SE by <= 1.5%.
- statsmodels raised a convergence warning on 9 of the 60 data sets; its estimates still matched the custom fit within the tolerances above.

**CR2 and Bell-McCaffrey df vs a dense direct implementation:** SE max abs diff 1.9e-16; df 4.6e-14. The dense implementation uses A_j = (I - H_jj)^(-1/2), G_j = (I - H)[:, j] A_j X_j M c and B = G'G.

**HC1 and two-stage analysis vs statsmodels OLS:** HC1 SE max abs diff 4.2e-16. Two-stage estimate 5.6e-16 and SE 2.5e-16; df identical.

## Reproduction

Steps (results do not depend on K):

1. Run `python rct_sim_v2.py --shard k --nshards K` for k = 0..K-1.
2. `python rct_sim_v2.py --merge`.
3. `python rct_sim_v2.py --manifest`.
4. `python validate_v2.py`.
5. `python summarise_v2.py`.
6. `python figures_v2.py`.
7. `python make_methods_v2.py`.

The run used 18 single-thread workers on a local Windows host (numpy 2.4.6, scipy 1.17.1, pandas 2.3.3, statsmodels 0.15.0), taking 682 s wall-clock for the slowest shard.
